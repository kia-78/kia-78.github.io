Bio

Hello, I’m Reza Badiei.

I’m an Electronics Engineer at ASHA Beam Profile with an M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering, specializing in Digital Electronic Systems.

My work spans custom SoC interface PCB design, microcontroller firmware, embedded Linux and camera systems, FPGA development, and CUDA-based computing. My research has focused on energy- and resource-efficient hardware for spiking neural networks.
